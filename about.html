ABOUT US

St. Maria Gorreti girls’ secondary school is a catholic based school located 1.5 km from Fort Portal city
Kasese highway on Virika Hill.

The school’s roots trace back to when Catholic missionaries arrived in Tooro region.
Fr. Achte opened St. Cecilia Elementary Vernacular School for girls a few years after 1895,
which later evolved into St. Maria Goretti Girls’ secondary school.

Founded under the Catholic Diocese of Fort portal,
the institution operates with a strong Catholic foundation,

holding regular spiritual milestones such as feast day celebrations led by local bishops.
